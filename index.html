About AlphaLab

AlphaLab is an AI research group at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), led by Prof. An Zhang at the School of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (SAIDS). The lab was officially established in September 2025.

We work at the frontier of Foundation Models — large language models and diffusion models — with a focus on understanding their capabilities, reasoning, and alignment. Our research bridges theoretical insights with real-world applications in Personalization, Agentic AI, and Trustworthy AI.

We collaborate closely with leading research groups worldwide and actively publish at top-tier venues including NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, KDD, ACL, SIGIR, WWW, and CVPR. We warmly welcome passionate researchers to join our team.
